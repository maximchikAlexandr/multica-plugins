# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

Harassment, discrimination, insults, threats, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to `maximchik.alexandr@yandex.ru`. Reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
